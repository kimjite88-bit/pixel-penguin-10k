ENGLISH (GLOBAL MARKET DESCRIPTION)

A tribute to timeless pixel art craftsmanship on Verychain. Inspired by the iconic legacy of classic pixel PFP collections on the blockchain, Very Penguin Labs created PIXEL PENGUIN (Pixel Penguin 10K) as an original 10,000-piece 48×48 pixel art PFP NFT collection native to the Verychain ecosystem. Featuring 11 hand-crafted 1-of-1 animated Mythic originals (#1–#11) and 9,989 algorithmically verified generative penguins across 210 original traits—including Korean royal heritage attire—all 10,000 penguins faithfully preserve a locked 1-pixel-1-color silhouette, existing purely as authentic PFP collectibles. On Verychat, they are prominently displayed as verified PFPs, making PIXEL PENGUIN owners instantly recognizable.
